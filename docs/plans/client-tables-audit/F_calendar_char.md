# F_calendar_char: calendar, character and skill tables

Builds: retail 12.1.5.70391, WoW: Forever 1.60.1.70374, Classic Era 1.15.9.70003. Counts come from those CSVs and from `data\quests.jsonl` (35,544 retail quests) and `data\forever\quests.jsonl` (5,097). Per-column detail is in `F_calendar_char-columns.csv`, per-bit in `-bits.csv`, per-table verdicts (all 94 tables) in `-verdicts.csv`.

## Summary

1. **The calendar side is already decoded correctly and almost fully used.** All 2,176 packed `Holidays` dates (2,041 retail, 107 Forever, 28 Era) decode with no field out of range and no bit above bit 28; Noblegarden's dated rows fall on real Easter Sundays (2006-04-16, 2020-04-12, 2021-04-04). `tools\HolidayTables.ps1` has the packing, stages, faction flags and region mask right. It misses only two Flags bits that change how Duration and Date are read, and the exact meaning of `CalendarFilterType`.
2. **No table links a quest to a holiday except five items.** Inbound references to `Holidays` are only `ItemSparse.RequiredHoliday`/`RequiredTransmogHoliday`, `PerksActivityXHolidays` and `TransmogHoliday`. Joined with `ItemSparse.StartQuestID`, 5 quest-starting items carry a holiday (retail; none on Forever): quests 43471, 43472, 47251, 47252 (Timewalkers' letters, row 693) and 52991 (Apple Sack, Hallow's End 324). 43472, 47251 and 47252 are tagged in our data; **43471 and 52991 are not in our data** although `QuestV2` has both ids. A holiday's dates therefore cannot tell which quests it makes available; the runtime calendar read plus our tags stay the source.
3. **The race-mask layout is proven.** `ChrRaces.PlayableRaceBit` is a bit index (-1 none, 0 to 33 used, 22 unused) into every 64-bit race mask the client keeps (`SkillRaceClassInfo`, `SkillLineAbility`, `ChrCustomizationReq`, `QuestV2CliTask.FiltRaceMasks_0/1`: two int32 words, `_1` holds bits 32 to 63). Proof: Racial-Dwarf = 0x804 = Dwarf + Dark Iron; Racial-Orc = 0x2002 = Orc + Mag'har; the Language-Common 64-bit mask decodes to exactly the 16 Alliance rows incl. High Order Skyborne. Our `qcRaceBits` equals Blizzard's bits 0 to 10 (Human to Draenei) and differs from bit 11 up (ours Worgen 11, Pandaren 12 ...; Blizzard Dark Iron 11, Vulpera 12, Mag'har 13, Mechagnome 14, Dracthyr 15/16, Earthen 17/18, Haranir 19/20, Worgen 21, Pandaren 23/24/25, Nightborne 26, Highmountain 27, Void Elf 28, Lightforged 29, Zandalari 30, Kul Tiran 31, Skyborne 32/33). Five races are faction-split in Blizzard's masks and merged into one bit in ours.
4. **The class mask is already Blizzard's.** `1 << (ChrClasses.ID - 1)` is confirmed by Forever's `TalentTab.ClassMask` (3 tabs per class, masks 1 2 4 8 16 64 128 256 1024 for classes 1 2 3 4 5 7 8 9 11) and by `SkillRaceClassInfo`. Retail has two non-playable classes (14 Adventurer, 15 Traveler); Forever and Era have 9 classes (no Death Knight, Monk, Demon Hunter, Evoker).
5. **Forever's playable races are 10 rows: the 8 classic races plus Skyborne 95 (High Order, Alliance) and 96 (Windshaper, Horde).** Retail 12.1.5 carries the same rows as `TBD NPC Race 1/2`. ChrRaces `NPCOnly` (Flags bit 0) clear selects exactly those 10 on Forever and the 8 on Era. `Read-QuestCache.ps1`'s non-retail test (Flags 0x400000) picks the same rows there but is set on 19 unrelated retail rows, so it is a coincidence, not a flag meaning.
6. **Profession expansion structure lives in `SkillLine`:** 14 base lines each with 12 children, `ParentTierIndex` 4 Classic, 5 Outland ... 12 Shadowlands, 13 Dragon Isles, 14 Khaz Algar, 15 Midnight, named via `ExpansionNameSharedStringID`. The 524 skill-gated task rows sit on tier 10 Legion (239), 11 Kul Tiran (172), 12 Shadowlands (52), base Fishing (44), Jewelcrafting (12), Tailoring (4), Classic (1). Forever has its own new tier-4 lines (2937 to 2948, one per profession; Test Profession 2933/2934; Poisons 2988) and its `PlayerCondition` rows already require them (Cooking 2939, Mining 2946, Tailoring 2948 ...), which our CMaNGOS-based Forever profession data cannot see.
7. **Nothing here is a localisation source we lack.** `GlobalStrings`, `HolidayNames`, `HolidayDescriptions`, `ChrRaces`, `ChrClasses` and `SkillLine` names are English in the CSV; the game supplies all 11 locales at runtime (and `_G` constants, which the addon already reads).

Earlier review (`docs\plans\client-tables-review.md`): no contradiction of substance. Row counts differ because it read 12.1.0.69933: Holidays 799 vs 811 here, HolidayDescriptions 280 vs 235 (Forever 27 vs 17), EventSchedulerEvent 43 vs 45, ScheduledInterval 295 vs 298.

## Holidays (retail 811 rows, Forever 22, Era 10)

Columns in `F_calendar_char-columns.csv`; method for each: Blizzard enums in `CalendarConstantsDocumentation.lua` (identical in the retail and Forever UI except one extra Forever flag), WoWDBDefs comments, rows.

- **Date_0..25**: bits 0-5 minute (63 any), 6-10 hour (31 any), 11-13 weekday (7 any), 14-19 day-1 (63 any), 20-23 month-1 (15 any), 24-28 year-2000 (31 any). Retail: 88 dates with any-year, 17 any-month, 9 any-day, 9 with a weekday; years 2004 to 2030.
- **Duration_0..9**: hours per stage, with `CalendarFlags_n` 1 Alliance / 2 Horde (3 both) marking the stages the calendar shows (first to last flagged). **Looping** repeats the stage sum.
- **Flags** (Blizzard `HolidayFlags`): 1 IsRegionwide, 2 DontShowInCalendar, 4 DontDisplayEnd, 8 DontDisplayBanner, 16 NotAvailableClientSide, 32 DurationUseMinutes, 64 BeginEventOnlyOnStageChange, 128 StagesUseDatetimes (**Forever's client only**). Retail rows use bits 0-3 (13: 580 rows, 1: 144, 3: 42, 15: 20); Forever 0/1/3; Era 3, 14, 15, 34 (bit 5 = 32 on rows 1498 and 1544). Neither 32 nor 128 is in any current retail or Forever row, so `HolidayTables.ps1` is right today; it would silently misread a row that sets them.
- **CalendarFilterType**: Blizzard `CalendarHolidayFilterType` 0 Weekly, 1 Darkmoon, 2 Battleground; any other value is a plain holiday. Retail uses 255 (552 rows), Forever uses 3 (13 rows) for the same events (Winter Veil 141: 255 on retail, 3 on Forever). qcHolidays' `filter=` agrees (Darkmoon 263/264 = 1, Stranglethorn Fishing 301 = 0).
- **Region**: mask of `Cfg_Regions.Region_group_mask` groups (bit n-1: 1 US, 2 KR, 3 EU, 4 TW, 5 CN, 6 events, 7 test, 8 third-party test, 9 development, 10 QA, 11 QA/CS); 99 retail rows are 0 (every region); 4 (EU) 166, 2 (KR) 160, 24 (TW+CN) 158, 961 (US + groups 7-10) 131. Rows of one event are duplicated per region and year, so reading the wrong row gives other dates.
- **Not decodable**: `Priority` (GUESS), `TextureFileDataID_0..2` (banner per start/ongoing/end, LIKELY), `WorldStateExpressionID` (Era CSV only).
- **Forever calendar**: 22 rows, 16 names. Names and IDs: 62 Fireworks Spectacular (region 1), 141 Winter Veil, 181 Noblegarden, 201 Children's Week, 263/264 Darkmoon Faire, 283-285 Call to Arms (old AV/WSG/AB), 301 Stranglethorn Fishing Extravaganza, 321 Harvest Festival, 324 Hallow's End, 327 Lunar Festival, 335 Love is in the Air, 341 Midsummer, 1840-1842 Call to Arms rotation (looping, 168+336 h), and four unnamed rows 1, 161, 281, 1879 (1879: Nov 15, 1464 h, Flags 3 = hidden from the calendar). Forever has none of Brewfest, Day of the Dead, Pilgrim's Bounty, Pirates' Day, Moonkin Festival, Trial of Style, anniversaries. Its dates for movable holidays end in 2025 to 2026 (Children's Week 201 has no run after 2025; 263/264 have no date; Noblegarden 181 has Duration 0), so offline next-run dates are reliable only for the fixed-date ones.
- **Offline next runs (12 months from 2026-10-14)** from the retail table for the 24 `qcHolidays` entries: 19 have a run (Day of the Dead Nov 1, Hallow's End Oct 19, Pilgrim's Nov 22, Winter Veil Dec 15, Darkmoon Nov 1 ...; Brewfest 2027-09-20), 2 have no calendar row by design (Scourge Invasion, AQ War Effort), Stranglethorn Fishing is Forever-only, and Hearthstone's 10th Anniversary and Dastardly Duos have no run in the window. Forever: 8 of 24 (Winter Veil, Hallow's End, Harvest Festival, Love is in the Air, Lunar Festival, Midsummer, Noblegarden, Fishing Extravaganza).
- **Holiday rows with no qcHolidays entry** (shown in the calendar, 698 rows, mostly raid/event weeks): Timewalking Dungeon Event (44), raid weeks, PvP Brawls, Call to Arms, bonus events, Luminous Luminaries (1054/1062), Thousand Boat Bash (642), Glowcap Festival (648), **A Greedy Emissary** (1382, 1688, 1691-1693; 2025-07-01 for 28 days). Our data has two quests named "Greedy Emissary" (91283, 91392) with no holiday tag; the event ran in July 2025, so if they were event-only they may be unobtainable now (CHECK in game, or against the Wowhead record). Timewalking: 15 of our quests are in the "Timewalking Campaign" heading, untagged, but those are campaign quests rather than event weeklies.

## ChrRaces (retail 59, Forever 58, Era 9)

Bit order table (Blizzard `PlayableRaceBit` -> row IDs): 0 Human 1, 1 Orc 2, 2 Dwarf 3, 3 Night Elf 4, 4 Undead 5, 5 Tauren 6, 6 Gnome 7, 7 Troll 8, 8 Goblin 9, 9 Blood Elf 10, 10 Draenei 11, 11 Dark Iron 34, 12 Vulpera 35, 13 Mag'har 36, 14 Mechagnome 37, 15 Dracthyr H 70, 16 Dracthyr A 52, 17 Earthen H 84, 18 Earthen A 85, 19 Haranir H 91, 20 Haranir A 86, 21 Worgen 22, 22 unused, 23 Pandaren neutral 24, 24 Pandaren A 25, 25 Pandaren H 26, 26 Nightborne 27, 27 Highmountain 28, 28 Void Elf 29, 29 Lightforged 30, 30 Zandalari 31, 31 Kul Tiran 32, 32 High Order Skyborne 95, 33 Windshaper Skyborne 96.

- **Flags** decode from TrinityCore `ChrRacesFlag` (names LIKELY): 0x1 NPCOnly, 0x4 CanMount, 0x10 BindToStartingArea, 0x80000 IsAlliedRace ... (all 25 in the bits file). PROVEN by rows: NPCOnly on all NPC/companion rows; IsAlliedRace on 14 rows.
- **Forever differences**: retail rows are present but flagged NPCOnly for Goblin, Blood Elf, Draenei, Worgen, Pandaren, allied races; FactionID is 0 for several (Blood Elf, Draenei, Worgen, Pandaren, Nightborne, Lightforged, Vulpera). Skyborne: 95 allowed classes Druid, Hunter, Mage, Rogue, Warrior; 96 Druid, Hunter, Rogue, Shaman, Warrior (from CharBaseInfo; other cluster).
- **Our race data vs the client's**: the retail quest cache lists race IDs per quest (10,886 quests carry a list). For the faction-split races only one twin appears in most lists (faction-level restriction); our data additionally narrows by start zone (e.g. quest 9604 mask 0x400 Draenei only while the cache lists every Alliance race). So ChrRaces cannot add accuracy there: the narrowing comes from other sources.
- **StartingLevel** (10 for allied races, Dracthyr, Earthen; 1 for Haranir and Skyborne): no quest effect.

## ChrClasses / ChrSpecialization / ChrClassRaceSex

Class mask proven (above). `ChrClassRaceSex` is a per class-race-sex voice/sound table (84 rows, 52 Dracthyr or Death Knight), not a legality table; legal race-class pairs are in `CharBaseInfo` (308 retail rows, 56 Forever). `ChrSpecialization` Flags follow TrinityCore's `ChrSpecializationFlag` (Caster 1, Ranged 2, Melee 4, DualWield 0x10, PetOverride 0x20, Recommended 0x40); no quest is spec-gated.

## SkillLine family

- `SkillLine.CategoryID`: 5 Attributes, 6 Weapon, 7 Class, 8 Armor, 9 Secondary, 10 Languages, 11 Professions, 12 Not Displayed, 222 Runes (names from the Forever/Era `SkillLineCategory` table; retail lacks it, and also uses 27).
- `ParentSkillLineID` + `ParentTierIndex` + `ExpansionNameSharedStringID`: see summary 6. Used today for the profession bit (`Sync-QuestProfessions.ps1`); the tier is not read. SharedString names 953 Classic, 954 Outland, 955 Northrend, 956 Cataclysm, 957 Pandaria, 958 Draenor, 959 Legion, 960 Kul Tiran, 961 Shadowlands, 962 Dragon Isles, 989 Khaz Algar, 1121 Midnight.
- `SkillLine.Flags` (TrinityCore `SkillLineFlags`): 0x8 only on the 15 retail class lines, 0x1000 only on the 11 primary professions, 0x4000 only on the 13 Fishing lines, 0x2000 on Racial-Worgen and Racial-Goblin; the rest are names without a test.
- `SkillRaceClassInfo`: rows give which classes and races may have a skill; `ClassMask` -1 = all, `RaceMasks_0/1` as above, `Availability` 1 on every Racial and Riding row, `MinLevel` for Riding (8 to 10; Forever 20 and 40), `SkillTierID` points at `SkillTiers`, which neither build ships (CANT-DECODE).
- `SkillLineAbility` (17,990 / 7,834 / 6,143 rows): spell-to-skill with recipe ranks; no quest link.

## Opportunities, ranked

1. **Add a by-name race map checked against Blizzard's bit order** (both games). Our masks stay as they are (deliberately 32-bit, one bit per race); the proof of the layout lets the importer, `Add-TaskQuests` and `QuestV2CliTask.FiltRaceMasks_0/1` readers share one mapping and a test that fails if a race is added without a bit. 83 distinct retail race masks and 5 faction-split races are affected. Cost low; value: protects the next new race (Skyborne on retail is the live example: bits 32/33, our bit 26).
2. **Quest 43471 and 52991 are missing from our data** (retail): both are quest-starting items with a holiday requirement and exist in `QuestV2`. Check them in game or on Wowhead and add or reject. 2 quests.
3. **Guard in `Compare-ClientTables.ps1` (step 2b)**: warn when a `Holidays` row used by `qcHolidays` has Flags 32 or 128, or a new `CalendarFilterType` value. Zero rows today; cost a few lines.
4. **Profession tier from `SkillLine`**: tag each profession task by expansion line with names from SharedString (localised by the game), and give Forever's tier-4 lines to the profession pass. 524 retail task rows with a skill gate (239 Legion, 172 Kul Tiran, 52 Shadowlands); Forever `PlayerCondition` rows with new-line skills (Cooking 2939 ...). Needs the PlayerCondition cluster for the Forever half.
5. **Offline next-run dates for holidays** (retail 19 of 24, Forever 8): a "returns on ..." tooltip line that works without opening the calendar; the table is wrong for Forever movable events after 2025, so label by source.
6. **"A Greedy Emissary" (2 quests, 91283/91392)**: possible event-only quests from July 2025 with no holiday tag; CHECK.
7. Not opportunities: `ChrCustomizationReq.ReqQuestID` (489 quests, none in our data), `Holidays` links to quests (none), `GlobalStrings`/`HolidayNames` localisation (English only), `SkillRaceClassInfo` (no quest depends on racial skills), `ChrClassRaceSex` (sounds).

## Things that look like they should exist but do not

- A quest-to-holiday table (retail or Forever). The calendar only knows event windows; quests are tied to events in TrinityCore's `game_event_creature_quest` or by item (`StartQuestID`), never in the client.
- `SkillTiers` (rank ceilings per skill tier) is referenced by `SkillRaceClassInfo.SkillTierID` and absent from both builds, so the maximum rank of a profession line cannot be checked from the client.
- Localised names in the CSVs (English only): all localisation must come from the running game.
- `SkillLineCategory` on retail (declared FK target missing).
